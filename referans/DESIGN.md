## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-clean, high-energy luxury dating experience tailored for a hyper-visual, discerning audience. Merging gallery-grade editorial precision with the magnetic pulse of contemporary nightlife, the aesthetic avoids stale tech tropes and somber tones in favor of sunlit, high-contrast clarity.

### Core Character & Atmosphere
- **Electric Youth Meets Curated Luxury:** Crisp porcelain canvas foundations juxtaposed with unapologetically vibrant sunset orange primary drivers.
- **Luminous Tension:** Sparkling neon cyan micro-accents and soft romantic rose highlights set against stark charcoal black typography, creating a sharp, editorial tension without heaviness.
- **Design Stance:** High-Contrast Editorial Pop. Ultra-lean structures, expansive whitespace, tight component discipline, and punchy, eccentric typographic scales provide immediate differentiation from standard algorithmic dating apps.

## Colors

The palette is engineered for vibrant legibility against a clinical, sun-drenched porcelain ground. Under no circumstances are muddy jewel tones, deep burgundies, or murky dark fills introduced.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Canvas & Backgrounds:** Pure Snow (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the base layer, with Porcelain Slate (`#F8FAFC`) establishing tonal card groupings and inset containers.
- **Primary Driver (`#FF5500`):** Energetic Sunset Orange. Used exclusively for primary call-to-actions, core swipe engagement states, verification anchors, and key kinetic focal points.
- **Secondary Accent (`#FF4D6D`):** Soft Rose Pink. Reserved for affection, matching moments, heart badges, and romantic confirmation indicators.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#00B4D8`):** Electric Neon Cyan. Applied strictly to live presence pulses, dynamic audio notes, status radar rings, and verification check badges.
- **Neutral Typographic Anchor (`#0F172A`):** Deep Charcoal Black. Delivers absolute optical contrast against pure porcelain, ensuring text and hairline iconography read as crisp luxury print rather than softened digital gray.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between the eccentric, expressive proportions of **Bricolage Grotesque** and the structural clarity of **DM Sans**.

- **Display & Headlines (Bricolage Grotesque):** Captures youthful magnetism and editorial charm. Used for candidate names, age displays, personality tags, match intros, and statement callouts. Its idiosyncratic glyphs give the interface an art-directed identity.
- **Body & Labels (DM Sans):** Delivers clean readability across multi-line bio prompts, compatibility breakdowns, messaging threads, and micro-metadata badges. Maintains legibility at high speeds and smaller screen viewports.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly disciplined 4px/8px modular spacing system anchors the luminous, airy layout, ensuring density remains breathable on handheld devices.

### Grid & Margins
- **Mobile First Focus:** Fixed 4-column layout on handheld screens with a `margin` of `1.25rem` (20px) to maximize edge-to-edge profile imagery while maintaining safe interactive thumb zones.
- **Tablet & Desktop:** Scales to an 8-column layout with a `margin-desktop` of `3rem` and centered phone-simulation viewports for desktop web parity.
- **Gutter Rhythm:** Internal component gutters hold strictly at `1rem` (16px) to maintain tension between visual prompt modules and candidate information blocks.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through soft, multi-layered ambient luminosity and translucent glassmorphism rather than muddy drop shadows.

- **Base Elevation (Level 0):** Flat `#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC` porcelain surfaces separated by high-precision hairline borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
- **Floating Cards (Level 1):** Main dating cards and bottom sheet overlays utilize extra-diffused ambient projection: `0px 12px 32px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05), 0px 2px 6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Interactive Action Nodes (Level 2):** Primary orange CTA triggers and floating swipe controls incorporate an energetic chromatic glow: `0px 10px 24px rgba(255, 85, 0, 0.28)`.
- **Glass Shrouds:** Modal overlays and photo meta cards utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `1px` translucent edge to preserve lightness over photography.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a structured, modern curvature (`roundedness: 2`) that pairs soft tactile handling with razor-sharp editorial definition.

- **Standard Elements (`rounded`):** Default inputs, micro-tags, system alerts, and messaging bubbles utilize `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Cards & Media Panels (`rounded-lg`):** Full-bleed bio modules, photo carousels, and dialogs utilize `1rem` (16px).
- **Action Triggers & Floats (`rounded-xl`):** Primary action buttons, swiping action nodes, and interactive story avatars leverage `1.5rem` (24px) for optimized ergonomics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Ignite):** Solid `#FF5500` background, pure white bold text (`label-lg`), `rounded-xl` geometry with chromatic orange elevation. Active state slightly scales down (`scale(0.97)`).
- **Secondary Interaction:** Translucent `#F8FAFC` background with a crisp 1px `#0F172A`/10% border and charcoal `#0F172A` text.
- **Micro / Icon Actions:** Circular 56px action nodes with white backdrops and specialized icon fills (Sunset Orange for super-likes, Rose Pink for matches, Charcoal Black for dismiss).

### Chips & Badges
- **Interest & Lifestyle Chips:** `#F8FAFC` background, 1px solid `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`, Charcoal Black text in `label-md`. Active selection toggles to solid `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Heart & Match Badges:** Soft Sweet Rose (`#FF4D6D`) tinted backgrounds (`12% opacity`) with high-saturation `#FF4D6D` iconography and bold counter text.
- **Pulse Indicators:** Glowing `#00B4D8` neon dots paired with a translucent expanding ring animation for live presence and active distance metrics.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Crisp `#F8FAFC` surface with inset padding (`space-md`), borderless in rest state, transitioning to a sharp 1.5px `#FF5500` outline upon focus. Text is strictly `#0F172A` body-md.
- **Radio & Selection Toggles:** Custom rounded squares (`rounded`) with a 1.5px charcoal contour; checked state fills with vibrant `#FF5500` featuring an ultra-crisp white check glyph.

### Profile & Discovery Cards
- **Dating Deck Cards:** Full `rounded-lg` containers with edge-to-edge photography, bordered by a barely perceptible translucent highlight ring. Editorial overlays fade upward from bottom-anchored soft gradients to guarantee Bricolage Grotesque headline readability.
- **Prompt Cards:** Pure white containers over `#F8FAFC` canvas with distinct charcoal typography quotes, offset by vibrant orange accent quotation marks.
